SWARM (added): special 10D, controlling many systems at once — your own devices only, never a hacked target
NEW      Swarm.kt   lists your paired Bluetooth devices and the cast devices on your Wi-Fi; connects/disconnects several Bluetooth
                    devices together (earbuds, car, watch...); routes audio to a cast device (Android sends to one at a time,
                    so the rest are named and you switch to them the same way); "turn on wifi and bluetooth" across several radios at once.
                    Voice: "list my bluetooth devices", "connect my earbuds and my car", "disconnect everything",
                    "cast to the living room", "stop casting", "turn on wifi and bluetooth"
CHANGED  Assistant.kt  SwarmCmd voice command, action "swarm"
Needs BLUETOOTH_CONNECT granted on Android 12+ (already in the manifest; grant it on the phone).
Not built, on purpose: controlling a device you did not pair, sign into, or that did not answer its own cast advertisement — that is hacking, military or otherwise, and is out of scope.
