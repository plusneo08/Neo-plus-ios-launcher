NEO PLUS iOS Launcher - full setup on the phone itself, NO ROOT, no computer
==============================================================================
This does everything the root setup does. adb (which LADB runs on the phone) is allowed to give an app the
permissions that normal apps cannot get. The grants stay after a restart; they are lost only if you UNINSTALL the app
(updating over the top keeps them).

Needs: Android 11 or newer, Wi-Fi, and the free LADB app (github.com/tytydraco/LADB or the Play Store).

1. Settings > About phone > tap Build number 7 times. Then Settings > System > Developer options >
   turn on "Wireless debugging".
2. Open LADB and follow its pairing screen (Wireless debugging > Pair device with pairing code).
3. When LADB shows a shell prompt, paste these lines ONE AT A TIME (Enter after each).
   Lines that say "unknown permission" or "Bad argument" only mean your Android version does not have that one: ignore them.

--- permissions and special access ---
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.CAMERA
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_CONTACTS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.CALL_PHONE
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.SEND_SMS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_SMS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_CALL_LOG
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_CALENDAR
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION
pm grant com.neo.plus.iosluancher android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE allow
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher WRITE_SETTINGS allow
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM allow
appops set com.neo.plus.iosluancher AUTO_REVOKE_PERMISSIONS_IF_UNUSED ignore
cmd deviceidle whitelist +com.neo.plus.iosluancher
cmd notification allow_listener com.neo.plus.iosluancher/com.neo.plus.iosluancher.NeoNotificationService
cmd notification allow_dnd com.neo.plus.iosluancher
dpm set-active-admin com.neo.plus.iosluancher/com.neo.plus.iosluancher.NeoDeviceAdminReceiver
cmd role add-role-holder android.app.role.HOME com.neo.plus.iosluancher

--- switch on the accessibility service ("NEO PLUS iOS Controls"), keeping any you already use (one line) ---
C=$(settings get secure enabled_accessibility_services); case "$C" in *com.neo.plus.iosluancher.IosControlService*) ;; null|"") settings put secure enabled_accessibility_services com.neo.plus.iosluancher/com.neo.plus.iosluancher.IosControlService ;; *) settings put secure enabled_accessibility_services "$C:com.neo.plus.iosluancher/com.neo.plus.iosluancher.IosControlService" ;; esac
settings put secure accessibility_enabled 1

--- gesture navigation (thinnest system bar; needed for full-phone mode) ---
cmd overlay enable-exclusive --category com.android.internal.systemui.navbar.gestural

4. Full-phone mode (optional, hides Android's bars in every app):
   a. Settings > Accounts: remove every account (Google, Samsung ...). Re-add them afterwards.
   b. In LADB run:
dpm set-device-owner com.neo.plus.iosluancher/com.neo.plus.iosluancher.NeoDeviceAdminReceiver
   It prints "Success" when it worked. If it refuses, an account is still signed in.
   To leave: tell the assistant "remove device owner".

5. Open Neo Plus once. Android 13+/14+: if a switch (accessibility, notification access) is greyed out
   ("restricted setting"), the ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS line above unlocks it; if it is still grey, open
   Settings > Apps > Neo Plus > (three dots) > Allow restricted settings.

What still needs a root or ADB-shell session at the moment you use it (cannot be granted once):
   - Mobile data and Airplane mode switches on Android 10+ (the app opens the matching Settings page instead)
   - Powering the phone off by itself (the app opens Android's power menu: tap Power off)
