NEO PLUS COMMUNICATION ASSISTANT
================================

The old Sleep, Work, Journey, Chill, Meeting and automatic-sleep modes are retired.
Use one explicit communication control instead:

  answer my messages and calls
  I'm back
  am back

While enabled, eligible messages are answered in the sender's latest language and incoming callers are handled through the configured call-assistant rules. When the owner says “I'm back”, NEO PLUS reports all retained events from the active period and turns the communication control off.

The catch-up report includes message content and replies, skipped or unresolved messages, other recorded notifications, call outcomes and available call transcripts. It hides codes and passwords. Existing logs and per-contact preferences are preserved.

Call rules remain available, for example:
  {"do":"rule","who":"Mum","action":"announce"}
  {"do":"conversation_setup","style":"short, friendly","brief":"take a message","info":"...","languages":"en-US,ny-NZ"}
